This experience shaped how I approach technology today. I value systems that are clear, organized, and reliable. I understand how frustrating poorly designed tools can be — especially for non-technical users — and I aim to build solutions that reduce that friction.

Whether it’s ensuring database accuracy, improving workflows, or supporting team communication, my goal is always to make systems easier and more effective to use.
